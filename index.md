## Who am I?
A NASA Einstein and KIPAC fellow at Stanford. I did my PhD at UChicago and the Kavli Institute for Cosmological Physics, and my undergrad at the University of Michigan (Go Blue!). Before that, I spent eighteen years growing up in a coastal city in South India. I pronounce my name just the way it is written (Da-ya An-buh-jug-uh-neigh). My full name in the Tamil script is written as <span lang="ta">தயா அன்பழகன்</span>. The phrase <span lang="ta">தயா</span> is a Sanskrit word for compassion, and <span lang="ta">அன்பழகன்</span> is derived from the Tamil word for beauty and affection.


## What do I do?

My main research program is focused on answering the following question: "What happened in the first moments after the Big Bang?" I build new datasets and modelling tools to answer this question from different avenues, to build up a more coherent and robust picture. See [my research overview](/research.md) for a bit more detail about what I work on.


## Working with students

I am always interested in working with students! I strongly believe in 1-on-1 mentoring of junior researchers, and this is a top priority to me. I've had the chance to work on [several projects](/cv/#mentoring) with fantastic undergrads and grad students at both UChicago and elsewhere (e.g. the [Baryon Imprints](/_projects/BaryonImprints.md) series, led by UChicago undergrads). If you are an undergrad or grad student interested in any of the topics I work on, whether you're here at Stanford/KIPAC or elsewhere, just drop me an email at [dhayaa@stanford.edu](mailto:dhayaa@stanford.edu)!
